CAREER GUIDE PRIVACY WEBSITE — SETUP
===================================
Files: index.html, deletion.html, style.css

1. Replace EVERY occurrence of REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL in index.html and deletion.html with your real monitored support email.
2. Verify every privacy statement against your released app, including AI/backend data sharing, notification tokens, storage, retention and children's access.
3. Implement and test account deletion for Firebase Authentication AND related Firestore records before claiming deletion is available. The deletion page is a request form via email only.
4. Publish this folder in a NEW public GitHub repository (do not touch the existing career-guide repo).
5. In the new repository: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
6. GitHub Pages will give a public HTTPS URL such as https://USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/ (the exact URL depends on your account and repo).
7. Open both privacy and deletion pages on mobile and confirm contact links work before submitting to Play Console.

This is a draft, not a legal certification or proof of Play Store compliance.
