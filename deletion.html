Account & Data

Request account deletion.

You can contact the Career Guide support team to request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Setup required: This is a request channel, not an automated deletion system. The developer must configure a working, verified deletion process before publishing.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address associated with your Career Guide account, with the subject “Career Guide account deletion request”. Do not send your password.

Email deletion request

What data is covered?

Your request should cover your Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore profile, including name, email, phone, city, bio, skills and learning-progress fields, subject to applicable legal or security retention obligations. The actual deletion workflow must be implemented and verified.

Questions?

Contact careerguidesupport69@gmail.com. The developer must confirm response and deletion timelines before publication.
