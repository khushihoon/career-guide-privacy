Privacy & Trust

Your privacy matters.

This page explains how the Career Guide mobile app collects, uses and handles personal information.

Draft effective date: 30 October 2026
Pre-publication draft: The developer must verify all data practices, complete the support email, and confirm the deletion process before using this page in Google Play Console.

01. Information we collect

When you register, the app may collect your name, email address, phone number, city and Firebase user identifier. It also stores profile information such as your bio, skills, application count, completed roadmap steps and XP points. Your authentication credentials are processed by Firebase Authentication; we do not describe passwords as stored in our Firestore user profile.

02. How we use information

We use this information to create and authenticate accounts, provide your profile, track learning progress, support career guidance features, and maintain app functionality. The app requests notification permission and may obtain a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for notifications.

03. Firebase and third-party services

Career Guide uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms. Some app features may involve additional AI, job-data or backend services; their precise data handling must be reviewed before publication. See Firebase Privacy information.

04. Data sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app. This draft does not claim that data is never shared: any additional integrations and data transfers must be verified against the deployed application before publication.

05. Security and retention

We use Firebase-backed infrastructure and aim to take reasonable steps to protect personal information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Account and profile data may be retained while your account is active, and deletion requests are handled according to the verified deletion process and any legal retention requirements.

06. Your choices and rights

You may update certain profile information in the app. You may request account and associated-data deletion using the instructions on our Account Deletion page. You can also manage notification permissions through your device settings.

07. Children's privacy

The app's intended audience and age-related controls must be confirmed before publishing. If the app is directed to children or allows children to use it, additional requirements may apply.

08. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and its data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

09. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email careerguidesupport69@gmail.com.
